# Big Bang Duel

## The idea

Big Bang Duel began as a simple, fast, browser-first idea: a strategy card game that could be picked up in seconds, but still feel rich enough to reward repeated plays.

The original goal was to make the first session feel immediate. A player could step in as a guest, see the core loop quickly, and choose whether to keep exploring the game or continue with a more personal account path later.

## Why guests matter

The project was designed around a low-friction experience. Guest access removes the need for a full account setup before the player understands the game’s value. It keeps the first click playful and focused on action, while the Google sign-in flow supports returning players without making the experience feel heavy.

## The rules and progression

The design centers on a short duel loop: decide on a class, build a strategy around your chosen tools, and manage tempo against an opponent. Each match is meant to teach the player the game’s rhythm quickly, while progression rewards better decisions and stronger card combinations over time.

The project also introduced a clear hierarchy of classes, tactical choices, and progression goals. The challenge was not just making the game fun in isolation, but making the systems readable from a phone-sized screen without overwhelming the player.

## The technical challenge

The main difficulty was balancing accessibility with depth. A fast tactical game needs to feel responsive, readable, and easy to grasp on mobile, while still giving enough room for meaningful decisions and a sense of progression.

This meant keeping the interface clear, reducing friction, and ensuring the guest-first flow and account path felt coherent instead of bolted together.

## Hugo's role

Hugo shaped the product direction across the game concept, UI flow, player onboarding, and the deployment-focused polish needed to make the experience feel complete. The work focused on practical product decisions as much as on the game systems themselves: how a player enters, learns, and returns to the game.

## The result

Big Bang Duel is meant to feel approachable, quick to understand, and memorable on first contact. It brings together a clear strategic fantasy, a guest-first onboarding model, and a sustainable progression path that can continue to grow as more features are added.
